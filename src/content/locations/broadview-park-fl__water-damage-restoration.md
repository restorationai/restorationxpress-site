## Why Broadview Park Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of the housing stock here predates modern plumbing codes, which means galvanized supply lines and cast iron drain stacks are still common under the slab. Those materials corrode from the inside out over decades, and a pinhole leak can run for weeks before a homeowner spots a damp baseboard or a musty smell near a closet. Add South Florida's wet season, with afternoon thunderstorms that drop heavy rain in short bursts, and flat lots that drain slowly toward the canal system running through Broward County, and you get a pattern we see constantly: water intrusion that starts small and spreads fast because the ground has nowhere to send it.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Broadview Park

The first call is almost always about standing water, in a kitchen, a garage, or under a slab. We start with extraction using truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on volume, then move to assessment: moisture mapping with penetrating meters to find out how far water traveled under flooring and behind baseboards. Terrazzo and tile floors common in this area's older homes can trap moisture underneath for weeks if the slab isn't dried properly, so we set drying equipment based on readings, not guesswork, and track progress daily until materials hit dry standard. Drywall that's wicked water more than a couple inches above the flood line typically comes out rather than being dried in place, which keeps mold from establishing in the wall cavity later.

## Broadview Park Insurance Coordination

Because Broadview Park sits in a flood-prone part of Broward County, a lot of the water losses we see involve a split between a standard homeowners policy and a separate NFIP flood policy, and which one applies depends heavily on whether the water came from inside the home (a burst pipe or appliance failure) or rose from outside (storm surge, canal overflow, or surface flooding). We document the source of the loss carefully, with photos and moisture logs, because that distinction often determines which policy pays. We'll walk you through what we're seeing on-site so you're not guessing which claim to file.
