## Why Washington Park Properties See Water Damage Issues

Washington Park sits in the flat, low-lying terrain typical of South Florida, where storm drainage relies heavily on gravity and a high water table that leaves little room for runoff to go anywhere but sideways, into yards, garages, and ground-floor living space. Afternoon thunderstorms that build quickly during the wet season can dump more rain in an hour than aging stormwater systems were designed to move, and slab-on-grade construction, common throughout the area, means water intrusion often travels under flooring before a homeowner notices a problem at the baseboards. Homes built before the 1980s frequently still have original cast iron or galvanized plumbing, both of which are prone to pinhole leaks and slow seepage that can go undetected inside wall cavities for weeks before staining or odor gives it away.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Washington Park

The first step on any call is identifying the water category and shutting off the source, whether that is a supply line, a roof penetration, or groundwater intrusion from heavy rain. From there we extract standing water, pull affected baseboards or flooring where needed to check for saturation underneath, and set containment if the loss is isolated to part of the home. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with moisture readings taken daily on framing, subfloor, and drywall so drying equipment can be adjusted or removed as materials actually reach dry standard, not on a fixed schedule. In slab homes, that often means running air movers and dehumidifiers longer in sections where water had nowhere to drain and sat against concrete for an extended period.

## Local note

One detail that catches homeowners off guard in this part of Florida: because so many homes sit on slab foundations with minimal subfloor void, water from a slow leak doesn't always pool where you'd expect. It can travel laterally under tile or vinyl plank flooring and show up as a stain or soft spot several feet from the actual source, which is why we check moisture levels across an entire room rather than just the area that looks wet.
