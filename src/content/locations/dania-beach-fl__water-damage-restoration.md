## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Dania Beach

We start with water extraction using truck-mounted and portable pumps to pull standing water out before it migrates further into subfloor and wall cavities. From there we use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map how far water has traveled behind baseboards, under tile, and into insulation, since visible wet spots rarely mark the true boundary of the damage. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration: air movers and dehumidifiers are placed based on the moisture readings, not guesswork, and we re-check readings daily until materials return to a normal moisture content. For homes with terrazzo or concrete slab flooring common in this area, drying time often runs longer than a typical drywall job because dense materials release trapped moisture slowly, so we adjust equipment placement and monitoring schedules accordingly rather than pulling out early.
