## Why West Park Properties See Water Damage Issues

West Park sits on the flat, low-lying terrain typical of eastern Broward County, where the water table sits close to the surface for much of the year. Most homes here are built slab-on-grade, which means water from a failed washing machine line or a slow slab leak doesn't just pool on top of the floor, it can wick up through the concrete and into drywall and baseboards before anyone notices a problem. Add in South Florida's summer storm pattern, heavy afternoon rain that arrives fast and drains slowly on saturated ground, and you get two very different but equally common causes of water loss: interior plumbing failures in older homes and storm-driven intrusion around roof flashing, window seals, and door thresholds. Homes built in the 1960s and 1970s, which make up a good share of the city's housing stock, often still have original galvanized or cast iron supply lines that are decades past their expected service life. Humidity is the other factor that changes the math. Once water sits in a wall cavity or under flooring in this climate, mold colonization can start within a day or two, not a week, so the drying window is shorter than it would be in a drier region.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in West Park

We start with extraction, pulling standing water off floors and out of carpet pad with truck-mounted or portable extractors before it has a chance to migrate further into the slab. From there we map moisture with meters and, where needed, thermal imaging to find out how far water has traveled under tile, laminate, or baseboards, because on slab foundations the visible wet line is rarely where the water actually stops. Drying equipment, air movers and dehumidifiers, gets placed based on that moisture map and the ambient humidity in the room, since South Florida's baseline humidity means dehumidification often has to work harder and run longer than a textbook drying plan would assume. We check and document drying progress daily and follow the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying as our benchmark for when a space is actually dry, not just dry to the touch. Any materials that can't be reasonably dried, like saturated drywall or delaminated subfloor, get removed and documented before reconstruction starts.

## Insurance Documentation for West Park Claims

Water losses tied to storm intrusion and those tied to a plumbing failure are often treated differently by carriers, so we document the source of the water, the extent of the affected materials, and our moisture readings from day one. That documentation, photos, meter readings, and a written drying log, gives your adjuster what they need to evaluate the claim without back-and-forth delays.
