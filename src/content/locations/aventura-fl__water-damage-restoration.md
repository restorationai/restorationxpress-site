## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Aventura

We start with extraction using truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on the volume of standing water, then move to moisture mapping with penetrating meters to find out how far the water traveled behind walls and under flooring. In condo units, that often means checking adjoining walls and the unit below for moisture before we can tell an owner or association rep how far the damage actually spreads. Structural drying equipment gets placed and calibrated for the ambient humidity, which in Aventura runs higher year-round than most of the country, so dehumidifiers often need to run longer here to pull the same amount of moisture out of drywall and subfloor. We document every reading throughout the dry-out so there's a clear record for whoever ends up paying the claim.

## Aventura Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most water losses in Aventura's condo buildings involve two insurance policies at once: the owner's individual unit policy and the association's master policy, and the line between what each one covers depends on where the water originated and what the governing documents say about "bare walls" versus "all-in" coverage. Associations also frequently require a licensed contractor's documentation before approving access to shared risers, chases, or common-area plumbing, which can slow down a repair timeline if it isn't handled correctly from the start. We photograph and log moisture readings as we go so that documentation is ready whether it needs to go to the unit owner's carrier, the association's carrier, or both.
