## Why Franklin Park Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of Florida's housing stock, including homes in and around Franklin Park, sits on concrete slab foundations rather than basements, which changes how water behaves once it gets inside. Instead of pooling where you can see it, water tends to travel laterally under tile and vinyl flooring, following grout lines and slab seams toward interior walls. Add in afternoon thunderstorms that are part of the regular summer pattern across this part of Florida, along with air conditioning systems that run nearly year round, and you get two common failure points: yard and street drainage that backs up faster than it can be absorbed, and condensate lines or drip pans that quietly overflow inside a closet or attic for days before anyone notices a stain.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Franklin Park

The first step on any call is confirming the water source and shutting it off, whether that's a supply line, a failed appliance connection, or a roof penetration from storm wind.
