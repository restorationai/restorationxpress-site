## Restoration emergencies common in Franklin Park

South Florida's combination of heavy summer rainfall, hurricane season, and a high water table means water intrusion is the call we see most, whether it is a slow roof leak that's been feeding mold behind drywall for weeks or a storm surge of rain overwhelming a yard that can't drain fast enough. Humidity that rarely drops below the point where mold needs encouragement means a wet wall cavity can start growing colonies within 24 to 48 hours if it isn't dried properly. Air conditioning condensate lines, flat or low-slope roofing common on older ranch homes, and aging cast iron drain stacks are frequent culprits behind water damage calls that look minor on the surface and turn out to be much larger once we open the wall.

## Services we provide in Franklin Park

**Water damage restoration.** Water damage in Franklin Park often tracks into slab-on-grade flooring and under baseboards before a homeowner notices anything beyond a damp carpet. We extract standing water, pull affected baseboard and drywall where needed, and dry the structure using moisture readings rather than guesswork, following IICRC S500 drying principles.

**Fire and smoke damage.** Smoke residue clings to stucco, soffits, and HVAC ductwork differently than it does to older wood-frame construction. We clean and deodorize affected surfaces and coordinate with your contents and structural repair needs so the property isn't left smelling like the fire long after the flames are out.

**Mold remediation.** Florida's humidity does the opposite of what homeowners want after any water event. Our remediation work contains the affected area, removes compromised materials, and addresses the moisture source itself, not just the visible growth.

**Storm and wind damage.** Downed fencing, lifted shingles, and wind-driven rain intrusion around window and door flashing are typical after a tropical system moves through. We tarp and board exposed openings and begin drying before secondary damage sets in.

**Biohazard cleanup.** Sewage backups, trauma scenes, and other biohazard situations are handled with appropriate PPE and disposal protocols, discreetly and without cutting corners on containment.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Franklin Park

Much of Franklin Park's housing stock reflects the broader South Florida building boom, concrete block and stucco construction on a slab foundation, with little to no crawlspace or basement because of the region's high water table. That slab-on-grade design means water intrusion travels horizontally under flooring and baseboards rather than pooling in a basement, which changes how a drying plan has to be built. Plumbing in homes from that era is a mix of cast iron drain lines and copper supply, with some mid-century properties carrying galvanized or early plastic piping that is more prone to pinhole leaks and joint failure as it ages.

Sandy soil and a shallow water table mean poor-draining yards and foundation-adjacent moisture are common after sustained rain, and flood-zone designations affect a meaningful share of properties in this part of Florida. Structural repair and rebuild work typically requires a permit through the local building department, and Florida Building Code wind-load requirements apply to roofing and exterior envelope repairs given the region's hurricane exposure.
