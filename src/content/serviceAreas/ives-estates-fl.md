## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Ives Estates

Most homes in Ives Estates date to the postwar development boom of the 1960s and 1970s, built as concrete block and stucco (CBS) construction on slab foundations, a style chosen across Miami-Dade for its resistance to wind and termites. That means water intrusion tends to travel along the slab rather than drop into a basement or crawlspace, and it can sit against the block wall base long enough to wick upward into drywall and baseboards.
