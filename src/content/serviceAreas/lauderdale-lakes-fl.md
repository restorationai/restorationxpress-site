South Florida's wet season and hurricane season, roughly June through November, bring the highest volume of calls: wind-driven rain forcing its way through roof flashing and window seals, tropical downpours overwhelming aging storm drains, and the humidity that follows any water event, which accelerates mold growth on drywall and framing within 24 to 48 hours if materials are not dried promptly.
